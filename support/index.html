Skeasy support

Questions, problems or ideas about the Skeasy ski app? Email ranabrahami@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

Something went wrong on the slopes?

Open Skeasy, tap Send today's trip log on the home panel, and send the file to the address above with a line about what happened. The file holds today's GPS track and what the app did, so we can replay your day and find the problem.

Good to know

  • Skeasy covers Les 3 Vallées and Tignes–Val d'Isère in full detail, and about 450 more ski areas across Europe.
  • No signal on the mountain? Save the map on Wi-Fi first; routes and GPS work without a connection.
  • Skeasy is a guide, not a safety device. Always follow the resort's signs, closures and ski patrol.
